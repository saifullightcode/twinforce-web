Our Story

Built on Expertise, Driven by Results

TwinForce Solutions began in August 2022 as a Salesforce practice in Dhaka, Bangladesh, founded by Salesforce Architect Saiful Islam. In January 2024 it became TwinForce Solutions Limited, a software company that now brings together 35 Salesforce developers, software engineers, QA engineers and business specialists.

Today we design, build, test and support web applications, mobile apps, integrations and data solutions, and we have delivered 30+ projects for clients in the US, Australia, Europe and Canada. Our Salesforce work is delivered through our specialist company, TwinStack Solutions, a Salesforce Select Consulting Partner.

Our Salesforce Company

TwinStack Solutions

TwinStack Solutions is the dedicated Salesforce company of the TwinForce group and a Salesforce Select Consulting Partner. It handles Salesforce implementation, Nonprofit Cloud, integrations, data migration, Agentforce and managed support, and publishes three free apps on the Salesforce AppExchange. Clients get a specialist Salesforce team, backed by the people, processes and wider engineering capability of TwinForce.

Mission & Vision

Why we do this work, and where we are taking it.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality, results-driven software and Salesforce solutions that help our clients reach their business goals, through careful design, solid engineering, thorough testing and ongoing support, while growing the next generation of software engineers in Bangladesh.

Our Vision

To be a globally trusted software partner from Bangladesh, known for Salesforce depth through TwinStack and for dependable custom software, so that organisations of every size can build on technology that lasts.
